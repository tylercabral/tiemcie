02 — Who we are

Built from the inside, not consulted from the outside.

Tiemcie (pronounced TEEM-SEE) started with people who worked on the inside, solving real problems at a state lottery. The group has a collective 10 years of experience across finance, revenue collection, budgeting, financial reporting, data analytics, and research.

It all started with the PBCA (Profit Based Cost Accounting) project, which was the first effort to build a real data and reporting landscape for understanding lottery performance through data. From there, the team developed the FARM (Financial Analysis and Risk Management) unit, built to apply data, research, and machine learning to maximize sales and reduce risk.

Tiemcie is now aimed at solving problems on a broader scale by helping lottery agencies grow revenue and strengthen their retailer networks, reduce exposure to fraud and embezzlement risk, and find solutions with data, analysis, and research.

Tyler Cabral
Founder
